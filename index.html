GUIDING QUESTION

How can intelligent systems understand and act in the physical world?

ABOUT

Intelligence grounded
in the physical world.

I'm Mohamed, a Sudanese software developer with a research interest in computer vision, 3D perception, and embodied intelligence.

I am interested in how machines can turn visual observations into useful representations of their environment, reason about spatial structure, and connect perception with action.

My long-term direction is to develop AI systems that learn from interaction with the world. I approach this through independent study in machine learning, mathematical foundations, and practical experimentation.

CURRENT EXPLORATION

Visual representation learning, geometric reasoning, and the foundations of perception for autonomous systems.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

From seeing to understanding.

Three connected directions shape the questions I want to investigate.

01

Visual & spatial understanding

Learning to interpret images and recover the structure of a scene: geometry, depth, motion, and the relationships between objects.

Computer vision · 3D perception · Scene understanding
02

Embodied learning & robotics

Connecting perception, world models, and decision-making so that agents can learn through interaction and adapt to changing environments.

Embodied AI · Robotics · Reinforcement learning
03

Representations that generalize

Understanding how learned representations can transfer across tasks and remain useful when conditions differ from the training data.

Representation learning · Generalization · Multimodal learning

APPROACH

Understand. Implement. Evaluate.

I value clear questions, simple baselines, and careful evaluation. My preferred way to explore an idea is to understand its assumptions, implement a small version, and examine where it succeeds and fails.

Mathematical foundations

Linear algebra, probability, calculus, and optimization as tools for understanding learning systems.

Reproducible exploration

Readable code, controlled experiments, and honest documentation of assumptions and limitations.
